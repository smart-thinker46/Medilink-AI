# Patient Registration Workflow

version: 1.0
classification: INTERNAL

## Register a patient

To register a patient, open the Patient Registration module and choose **Register Patient**. Search by the patient's name, phone number, national ID, or medical record number (MRN) before creating a new record. This duplicate check is required so that an existing patient is not registered twice.

## Capture the registration details

Enter the patient's legal name, date of birth, sex, phone number, national ID or other identity details, address, next-of-kin details, and any required demographic or consent information. Use the values supplied by the patient or an approved identity document. Do not guess missing information; record it as unknown or follow the facility's verification workflow.

## Save and verify

Review the spelling, date of birth, phone number, identity number, and next-of-kin details with the patient. Submit the registration form only after the review is complete. The system creates or displays the patient's medical record number (MRN). Confirm the patient identity and MRN before proceeding to check-in, queueing, or another service module.

## Corrections and duplicate records

Do not create a second record to correct a mistake. Use the patient record's permitted edit workflow, and report suspected duplicate records to the records or facility administrator. Staff permissions and audit controls determine which fields can be edited after the patient has been checked in or queued to another module.
